# Measured reports

`system.json` was generated by `chess-ai doctor` in WSL2. `benchmark_before.json` and `benchmark.json` were generated from two local debug-profile checkpoints before and after shrinking the policy projection. The final checkpoint was trained from commit `2b89cb6` with clean source/config files. These files are real measurements on the specified machine; they do not measure playing strength. `model_summary.md` is generated by the benchmark command. Future training, evaluation, and bot ladder reports belong here only after actual runs.

`training/` contains run summaries generated by the training CLI. `evaluation/teacher_smoke_vs_random.json` records two attempted local baseline games from a five-position teacher smoke checkpoint: one completed draw and one unfinished game at the ply cap. This is not a strength estimate.

`datasets/` contains source, split, and teacher manifests for the small public-data experiment. `training/lichess-supervised-100.json` and `training/lichess-distill-256.json` record the two actual CUDA stages. `evaluation/lichess_supervised_vs_*.json` contains all ten attempted games against each local baseline, including unfinished games. `gtx1650_benchmark.json` measures inference of the distillation checkpoint. No Chess.com game or bot result is represented here.
