# Measured reports

`system.json` was generated by `chess-ai doctor` in WSL2. `benchmark_before.json` and `benchmark.json` were generated from two local debug-profile checkpoints before and after shrinking the policy projection. The final checkpoint was trained from commit `2b89cb6` with clean source/config files. These files are real measurements on the specified machine; they do not measure playing strength. `model_summary.md` is generated by the benchmark command. Future training, evaluation, and bot ladder reports belong here only after actual runs.
