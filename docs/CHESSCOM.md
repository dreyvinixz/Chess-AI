# Chess.com integration and safety

Browser integration is under development and is **not** exposed by the current CLI. On 2026-10-02, an anonymous Playwright session opened `https://www.chess.com/play/computer`. The live page showed `#board-play-computer`, pieces with classes such as `piece wp square-54`, coordinate labels, SAN mainline nodes in `[data-node].main-line-ply`, separate bot and guest username blocks, and a bot speech marker. After an observed manual `e4`, the page showed a legal bot reply in its SAN list. These selectors are centralized in `browser/selectors.py` and may change.

The draft adapter reconstructs a `python-chess` board from the SAN mainline, checks its piece placement against the DOM, and derives orientation from coordinate labels. `BotModeGuard` requires the exact computer URL, board ID, two distinct player names, a bot speech marker, and no open modal. The guard and parser have CPU tests for human-game URLs, missing bot signals, invalid placement, and flipped orientation. A live probe successfully read the initial bot position, name, and displayed rating. Its attempted click-to-move timed out without a recorded move, so browser move execution and autoplay are **not verified or enabled**. No Chess.com result is claimed.

The local assist mode records a human's actual moves; connecting it to this browser observer remains work. Autoplay will use only student model/search moves after execution and post-move reconciliation are verified. Stockfish may annotate a completed game offline. No Chess.com account credentials or cookies belong in this repository.
